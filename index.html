EcoMove AI Documentation

Carbon-Intensity Calculation Methodology

EcoMove AI estimates carbon intensity (measured in grams of CO₂ equivalent per kilowatt-hour or g CO₂/kWh) based on the energy source powering each charging station:

  • ☀️ Solar-powered (30 g/kWh): Near-zero life-cycle emissions from solar PV cells.
  • 🔋 Solar + Battery (45 g/kWh): Rooftop solar with energy storage batteries, providing clean power 24/7.
  • 🌬️ Wind / Verified Renewable (25 g/kWh): Backed by PPA contracts, wind farms, or certified green tariff certificates.
  • ☀️🔌 Solar + Grid Hybrid (350 g/kWh): Combination of on-site solar and standard grid electricity.
  • 🔌 Grid-Connected (420 - 820 g/kWh): Relies solely on state-level grid parameters. Coal-heavy states like West Bengal rank higher (~820g) than hydro/renewables-rich states like Kerala (~420g).
  • ❓ Energy Source Unknown: Handled using the conservative Indian grid average baseline (~700g CO₂/kWh).

How to Import CSV Datasets

To bulk load or update charging stations, format your files using the column headers shown below:

id,name,operator,address,city,state,latitude,longitude,chargerType,chargingPower,availability,energySource

Possible values for energySource: Solar-powered, Solar + Battery, Wind / verified renewable supply, Solar + Grid, Grid-connected, Energy source unknown.
